# Public Claims Boundary

## Allowed in Public Proof v1

The following are supported by the internal evidence package:

- AK-79 has a preregistered red-team benchmark program.
- Real Temporal SDK implementations were used in the internal comparison.
- Temporal matched AK-79 reference behavior on the visible critical and generalized preflights.
- A live AK-79 task/receipt path was verified internally.
- A real GitHub/CI visible preflight reached reliability parity on the tested task.
- AK-79 later reached one-call repeatability for that task.
- AK-79 did not demonstrate lower side-specific code burden on the single-class packaging comparison.
- The incremental-reuse code-burden hypothesis was killed by its preregistered AST threshold before Generation-2 external effects were run.
- Negative evidence was preserved.

## Not allowed

Do not claim:

- AK-79 beats Temporal.
- AK-79 is externally certified.
- AK-79 is production-proven.
- AK-79 has proven a commercial moat.
- AK-79 has proven market demand.
- AK-79 has passed the final hidden holdout.
- AK-79 VEIR is 95% or higher.
- Temporal VEIR is 75% or lower.
- AK-79 cost only USD 20 to build.
- AK-79 was independently proven to have been built in exactly 37 days.
- AK-79 is the only or best AI OS in the world.

## Evidence classes

AUTHOR_REPORTED means a result is reported by the author from internal runs. The supporting artifacts are not published here, so the result cannot be reproduced from this repository.

It does not mean external certification, and it does not mean independent verification by a third party.

FOUNDER_REPORTED means the fact comes from the founder and has not yet been independently reconstructed from external evidence.

CANDIDATE means a hypothesis is deliberately unproven and is eligible for falsification.
