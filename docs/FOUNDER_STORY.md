# Founder Story

This document separates founder-reported history from independently verified technical results.

## Founder-reported origin

The founder reports that AK-79 began from zero as an idea, using ChatGPT Plus from a phone.

A single personal laptop was added later.

The founder describes the concentrated build period as a little over one month.

This chronology is not independently audited in Public Proof v1.

## What can be said safely

A single founder used consumer-access AI tooling and a personal workstation to build a system capable of running a structured red-team program against a real Temporal SDK implementation.

That red-team program did not manufacture a victory.

It preserved where Temporal matched AK-79 and where AK-79's own hypotheses failed.

## The USD 20 comparison

OpenAI lists ChatGPT Plus at USD 20/month.

That figure is the subscription price only.

It is not the total cost of AK-79.

It excludes hardware, electricity, founder time, local compute, other subscriptions, and later tools/providers.

## Temporal context

Temporal announced a USD 550M Series E at a USD 12.55B valuation in September 2026.

The contrast is useful as a capital-efficiency story, not as an apples-to-apples ROI calculation.

## The public-proof story

The strongest statement is not:

"AK-79 beat Temporal."

The stronger, supportable statement is:

A single-founder system became sophisticated enough to preregister adversarial tests against a mature durable-execution stack, preserve competitor wins, expose its own defects, and stop a favored hypothesis when its own kill threshold fired.
